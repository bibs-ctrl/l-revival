# Felt Stop-Motion Cheat Sheet - Local Revival Tallow & Honey Body Wash

Quick reference for the creative briefing. Full details: `felt-stop-motion-script.md`.

## The Ad at a Glance

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, ~25s, 6 AI-generated clips, hard cuts.
- **Concept:** a felt miniature workshop shows how real soap is made, from farm to bottle to shower.
- **Voice-over:** 5 of 6 clips reuse audio from the founder's original video. Only the final line is newly recorded.

## Style (paste at the start of every prompt)

```
Handmade felt stop-motion animation, miniature workshop set, warm tungsten
lighting, frame-by-frame movement at 12 fps (on twos), visible wool-felt
fibers, visible hand stitching, soft shallow depth of field, tabletop
miniature scale, locked camera with small stepped moves. Palette: cream
felt, honey gold, amber brown, soft sage green. Tactile, imperfect, crafted.
```

**Negative:** smooth fluid CGI motion, glossy plastic, 3D render, photorealism, digital cartoon look, motion blur, any text/letters/logos, real human faces, neon, cold lighting.

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short, tactile, stepped movement (12 fps) | Fluid, digital-cartoon animation |
| Visible felt fibers and stitching | Smooth plastic or 3D-render surfaces |
| Warm light from camera-left, same in every clip | Change lighting or palette between clips |
| Use the same amber bottle reference in every clip | Let the AI redesign the bottle each time |
| Add label, text and sign in post | Let the AI generate any text or the label |
| Cream-apron felt artisan with button eyes | Detailed or realistic faces |
| Hard cuts between clips | Cross-dissolves |

## Sound

- Light, warm acoustic music, low in the mix (duck ~8 dB under VO).
- Tactile SFX: fabric rustle on pop-ins, wooden spoon clicks, snap-fit sounds, soft thud on the bottle bounce.
- Keep the VO in the foreground. Add room-tone pads between reused cuts.

## Brand and Claim Guardrails

- **No discount or offer** anywhere (the 50% offer has expired).
- Use only claims already in the founder's video: "real soap", "grass-finished tallow", "raw honey", "made by hand", "sourced from American farms".
- The two bowls are a visual highlight only. Don't imply the product has only two ingredients.
- No skin-result or health claims.
- Don't reuse the original's claims about private-equity brands or "detergent in a bottle".
- The real label always comes from a product photo, never from the AI.

## Assets to Prepare Before Generating

1. Real bottle photo, front, label visible.
2. Approved felt amber bottle (no label), used as the start frame for all clips.
3. Felt artisan character sheet (front and side).
4. Felt workbench and miniature workshop background plate.
5. Reference of the two bowls (honey and tallow) from the original video.
