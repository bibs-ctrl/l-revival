# Local Revival - Felt Stop-Motion Ad (~25s)

## How to read this file

- Each `CLIP` block is one AI-generated video to produce.
- `VO` is the voice-over line. `source` says **REUSED** (verbatim from the original video, with timestamp to cut the audio) or **NEW** (must be recorded).
- `prompt` is the text to paste into the generator. It always starts with the `GLOBAL STYLE` block.
- `edit` items (labels, text, logos) are added in post, never generated by the AI.
- Timestamps come from an automatic transcription of the original video. Listen to each cut before using it.

---

## GLOBAL STYLE (prepend to every clip prompt)

```
Handmade felt stop-motion animation, miniature workshop set, warm tungsten
lighting, frame-by-frame movement at 12 fps (animated "on twos", slight jitter
between frames), visible wool-felt fibers, visible hand stitching, soft
shallow depth of field, tabletop miniature scale, locked camera with small
stepped camera moves. Palette: cream felt, honey gold, amber brown, soft sage
green. Tactile, imperfect, crafted look. 9:16 vertical.
```

## NEGATIVE PROMPT (use on every clip)

```
smooth fluid CGI motion, glossy plastic, 3D render look, photorealism,
digital cartoon look, motion blur, text, letters, logos, watermarks,
real human faces, neon colors, cold lighting
```

## CONTINUITY RULES

- Same felt palette, same workbench, same warm light direction (key light from camera-left) in every clip.
- The amber bottle must look identical in every clip. Generate it once, then use it as the reference image for every clip (image-to-video).
- Real bottle details seen in the original video: amber glass bottle, pump top, cream swirl graphic on the label, small orange circular badge reading "Body Wash / Honey / Tallow". The felt bottle should echo the amber color and cream swirl, but the real label is composited in edit.
- Artisan character: simple felt figure, cream apron, no detailed face (two button eyes max). Same design across clips. It is a stand-in for the founder.
- No AI-generated text anywhere. Bottle label, "TALLOW + HONEY" and the final sign are added in edit.

## SOUND DESIGN

- Music: light, warm, acoustic; low in the mix.
- SFX (tactile): fabric rustle on every pop-in, wooden spoon clicks while stirring, small "snap-fit" sounds when pieces land, soft thud on the bottle bounce.
- VO is always in the foreground. Duck music by ~8 dB under the VO.
- Reused VO: cut directly from the original video's audio (timestamps above). Keep the same speaker and level across cuts, and add short room-tone pads between them so the cuts don't sound abrupt.
